About FindIT

FindIT is a campus digital lost-and-found bulletin board designed to give students, faculty, and staff one organized, searchable place to report, track, and return misplaced belongings on campus.

Our Mission

Replace lost items getting buried in random social media feeds and group chats with a central, searchable community bulletin.

Event-Driven App

Built with modern JavaScript event listeners, real-time filtering, localStorage persistence, and responsive UI updates without page reloads.

EDP - Group 4

Developed as a Final Project for Event-Driven Programming (EDP), November 2026, dedicated to serving our campus community.
