About WayBack

WayBack is a campus digital lost-and-found bulletin board designed to give students, faculty, and staff one organized, searchable place to report, track, and return misplaced belongings.

Our Mission

Stop lost items from getting buried in random social media feeds and group chats. WayBack is a central, searchable community bulletin.

Event-Driven App

Built with JavaScript event listeners, live filtering, localStorage persistence, and responsive updates without page reloads.

EDP - Group 4

Developed as a Final Project for Event-Driven Programming (EDP), November 2026, dedicated to serving our campus community.
